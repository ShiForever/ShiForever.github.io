About Me

Independent Study & Projects

Diffusion Models and Flow Matching: Theory and 2D Generative Experiments

Independent Study · Generative Modeling

  • Studied VAE, GAN, DDPM, DDIM, score-based models, continuous normalizing flows, and flow matching.
  • Developed an understanding of forward noising and reverse generation, the evidence lower bound, score matching, and the SDE/ODE perspectives of diffusion models.
  • Studied the construction of probability paths and velocity fields in flow matching.
  • Conducted exploratory two-dimensional experiments that transform a Gaussian source distribution into more complex target distributions.
  • Visualized the evolution of probability distributions over time to better understand the dynamics of generative models.
